About Hon. Echate

“A stronger Bomachoge Borabu where every voice counts.”

Hon. Duke Mainga Echate is seeking to represent the people of Bomachoge Borabu Constituency in the National Assembly: speaking for the constituency, shaping laws, and making sure public funds such as the NG-CDF reach the families and schools that need them. His promise is simple: listen first, serve fairly, and report back to the people.

What We Stand For

Education & Bursaries

Fair, open bursary support so every child can stay in school.

Jobs & Youth

Practical support for young people, small businesses and local enterprise.

Health & Water

Better clinics and reliable water for families across the constituency.

Farmers & Agribusiness

Backing farmers with fair markets, inputs and better rural roads.

Open & Accountable

Public reports on how NG-CDF and other funds are used.

Women & Persons with Disabilities

Fair access to opportunities, resources and leadership.

What does a Member of Parliament do?

An MP represents the constituency in the National Assembly, makes and reviews laws, oversees government, and works with the NG-CDF committee on local development.
